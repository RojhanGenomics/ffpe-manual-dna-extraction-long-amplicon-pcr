# Final Publication-Readiness Audit

**Project:** Manual FFPE DNA Extraction for Long-Amplicon PCR  
**Version:** 1.0.1  
**DOI:** 10.5281/zenodo.22091030  
**Audit date:** 2026-08-25

## Overall assessment

**Status: Research-artifact ready / publication-ready as a retrospective, DOI-archived research output.**

This repository is suitable for public scholarly use, citation, portfolio presentation, and research discussion. It should **not** be represented as a universal protocol recommendation, a controlled comparative validation study, or a claim that mineral-oil deparaffinization is novel.

## Evidence limitations that remain intentionally visible

- Complete sample-by-sample success rate is not available.
- Quantitative DNA integrity metrics are not established.
- Independent replicate statistics are not available.
- Comparative performance against commercial kits is not established.
- The evidence does not establish universal superiority.

These are **scientific limitations, not publication-readiness defects**, because they are explicitly disclosed and reflected in the claim boundary.

## Final editorial recommendation

The repository can be presented as a **DOI-archived retrospective research artifact / case study** documenting a practical optimization observed in a resource-constrained setting. A future journal manuscript would require a formal comparative experimental design and quantitative replication; that is a separate research phase and is not required for the present repository to be honest, citable, and researcher-ready.

## Final verdict

**READY — with the stated evidence boundary.**
