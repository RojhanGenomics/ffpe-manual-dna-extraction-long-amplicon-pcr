# Evidence Audit

## Evidence classes
- **Documented/reconstructed:** Manual FFPE DNA extraction workflow and the reported optimization steps supplied by the researcher.
- **Observed:** Before/after gel images showing longer PCR bands after optimization; ladder identified as 100 bp.
- **Reported:** Mineral-oil deparaffinization at the beginning, mechanical tissue fragmentation without liquid nitrogen, hot-start master mix plus enhancer buffer, and longer products up to approximately 700–1000 bp in a subset of samples.
- **Unknown:** Complete sample-by-sample success rate, exact quantitative DNA integrity metrics, independent replicate statistics, and comparative performance against commercial kits.

## Claim boundary
The evidence supports a practical optimization/feasibility claim. It does not establish universal superiority or invention of mineral-oil deparaffinization.
